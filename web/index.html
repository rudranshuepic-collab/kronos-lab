How to use this list

  1. Buy every pick at the next morning's open, the same amount of money in each. Skip any stock that opens above its "skip if above" price.
  2. Hold. Don't add the next evening's picks; one list at a time.
  3. Sell everything at the open on the sell date, whatever the price. Then take that evening's list.

This is the rule the Reality check tested. The emergency stop is extra protection and was not part of that test.
